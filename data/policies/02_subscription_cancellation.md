# Subscription Cancellation Policy

## Section 1: End-of-Cycle Cancellation (Default)
When a customer requests to cancel their subscription:
* The default action is cancellation at the end of the current billing cycle.
* The customer retains full access to all features until the current period expires.
* No further automated recurring charges will be initiated after confirmation.

## Section 2: Immediate Cancellation
* Immediate cancellation terminates account access immediately and is only applicable upon explicit customer request.
* Mid-cycle prorated refunds are not issued automatically; any prorating request must be evaluated under the Refund SLA policy.

## Section 3: Data Retention Post-Cancellation
* Customer configuration and workspace data are retained in read-only status for 30 days following cancellation before permanent deletion.

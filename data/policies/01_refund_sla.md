# Refund SLA & Dispute Policy

## Section 1: 14-Day Self-Service Refund SLA
Customers are eligible for a full refund on subscription renewals and initial purchases if requested within 14 calendar days of the charge date.
* **Auto-Approval Limit:** Refund requests with charges of $50.00 USD or less that meet the 14-day criteria may be automatically processed.
* **Processing Time:** Standard bank processing takes 3 to 5 business days to reflect on the customer's payment method.

## Section 2: Overcharge & Duplicate Billing
If a customer was billed twice for the same billing cycle or encountered an unauthorized duplicate charge:
* The duplicate charge is eligible for immediate 100% reimbursement regardless of the plan tier.
* Requires verification of duplicate invoice IDs.

## Section 3: Post-14-Day & Annual Plan Disputes
* Charges requested after 14 days from the billing event are strictly non-refundable via automated systems.
* Exceptional circumstances (e.g. medical hardship, unexpected annual auto-renewal within 48 hours of notification) require supervisor sign-off and must be routed to the Human Review Desk.

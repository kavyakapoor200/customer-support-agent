LangGraph State Machine Flow
1
Intake & Script Tagging 0ms
Detects English, Hinglish, or Devanagari Hindi; extracts amounts.
2
Retrieve Policy (Qdrant) ~15ms
Fetches relevant SaaS SLA rules (refund limits, cancellation terms).
3
Decide (Kev-0.8B) ~40ms
Fast System 1 classification yields calibrated probability distribution.
4
Threshold Gate & HITL Interrupt Deterministic
If ≥ 0.90 & ≤ $50 ➔ Auto-execute
If 0.60-0.89 OR > $50 ➔ Pause graph (Gradio Review Desk)
5
Draft & Verify (Groq) ~350ms
Mirrors customer's tone/language; verifies draft against policy invariants.
6
Execute & Audit Log Postgres
Executes mock tool & writes full trajectory to Postgres for evals.
